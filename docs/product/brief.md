# Product brief

## Request
    Bring the Abbaas Ledger in line with the refined final canvas at https://claude.ai/artifact/XpzsEF5ovaU7cB3wYp5bEi so the app matches it exactly: the new Home look with sends on the left of the centre line and payments on the right, the reworked Send and Receive forms without the note field, the after-saving preview, the Receive quick amounts or the over-payment warning, and the new save motion where Home grows from the form's button. Version one is integrated at f1ffe1d in /Users/Yuki/Claude/abbaas-ledger; sign-in, storage, totals, Undo, layout and install behave as before.

## Outcomes
-     At a glance the owner sees who is holding whose money and how much, in USD, from where the amount sits (the owner's box on the left, Abbaas's box on the right), without reading a plus or minus sign.
-     Recording a send (in USD, or in MVR at the rate of the day) or a repayment from Abbaas takes under a minute on a phone.
-     Every entry keeps its original currency, amount, rate and USD value, so the balance can be traced back entry by entry and an old entry never changes when a later rate differs.
-     The app runs on a phone browser from a free GitHub Pages URL and a free Supabase project, with no server for the owner to operate.

## Scope

### In scope
-     Email and password sign-in through Supabase Auth, asked once per phone: the session is kept and refreshed so later visits open straight to Home; the account reads and writes only its own rows.
-     Home: a two-box balance card (You left, blue; Abbaas right, teal) where only the box of whoever holds the other's money is filled with the amount, an All square state, totals sent and paid back, and the entry list.
-     Entry list on a centre line under an Entries heading with the count, grouped by day, newest first: each entry is a tinted card; sends sit left of the line (under You sent), payments from Abbaas sit right of it (under He paid back), each with the amount, who, its detail and, on the other side of the line, who holds what after it.
-     Send: a blue full-screen form opened from the Send button (bottom left) laid out as on the refined board: close and who holds what at the top, a USD/MVR switch with a sliding pill, the amount, in MVR a rate card beside a white In dollars card worked out live, paid-to chips chosen from a saved list or added, a date chip, and a white Send · $amount button at the bottom.
-     Receive: a teal full-screen form opened from the Receive button (bottom right) laid out the same way, with USD or MVR, amount, rate cards in MVR, a date chip and a white Receive · $amount button.
-     Motion from the refined board: the form grows as a circle from the tapped button; saving grows Home as a circle from the form button, then only the new entry arrives from its own side, the balance counts to its new value; a box drains while the other fills; reduced-motion settings are honoured. Opening the app plays Home's entrance, and a form's rows rise in after its colour fills the screen.
-     Light theme only, fluid layout from 360 px wide upward, capped at a centred 560 px column on wider screens, with room for Android's gesture bar.
-     Static hosting on GitHub Pages; Postgres schema, row-level security and migrations in the repo for Supabase.
-     Installable on Android: a web app manifest with name, icons and theme colour so Chrome's Add to Home screen opens the app full-screen.

### Out of scope
Applicability: applicable
Reason:     Version one is a private ledger between the owner and one person; anything that widens the people, currencies or platforms is deferred.
-     Ledgers with anyone other than Abbaas, or more than one counterparty.
-     A login for Abbaas, or sharing the ledger with him or anyone else.
-     Editing or deleting an entry after the Undo moment has passed.
-     Dark theme (designed on the exploration canvas, deferred for now).
-     Fetching exchange rates from the internet; the owner always types the rate.
-     Currencies other than USD and MVR.
-     Native iOS or Android apps.
-     Reminders, notifications, exports, receipts or photo attachments.
-     Offline entry with later sync beyond ordinary browser caching.
-     A sign-out control or account settings screen.
-     A note field on entries, an after-saving preview on the forms, quick amounts on Receive and a warning before an over-payment: the refined board leaves them out.
-     A Saved message and Undo after saving; deleting an entry is a later change.

-     Tapping Send opens the blue form as on the refined board: a close button and who holds what at the top, New entry · Send above the title Money sent for Abbaas, a USD/MVR switch with a sliding white pill, the amount in large type, in USD the line Dollars go straight onto the balance. -     Tapping Receive opens the teal form laid out the same way, titled Payment from Abbaas, with the currency switch, the amount, rate cards in MVR, the date chip and a white Receive · $300.00 button; there are no quick amounts, no note field, no after-saving preview and no over-payment note; saving puts the entry right of the centre line, and a payment larger than Abbaas holds empties his box and fills the owner's blue box with the difference.
-     After a save there is no Saved message and no Undo anywhere on Home: the new entry's card, arriving on its own side of the centre line with a ring in its colour, is the only feedback, and the stored entry stays stored.
-     Tapping the white Send or Receive button on a form presses it, then Home grows as a circle from that button's centre over the dimmed form; once Home fills the screen only the new entry moves, arriving from its own side (a send from the left, a payment from the right) with a ring in its colour that is never cut off, the balance counts to its new value, and a payment larger than Abbaas held drains his box while the owner's fills; with the device set to reduce motion, Home shows its end state at once.

-     Once each screen's entrance has played, every screen of the refined board fills the phone's width from 360 px upward with no horizontal scrolling and no element past the screen's edge, keeps the Send and Receive buttons clear of Android's gesture bar, stays a centred column no wider than 560 px on wider screens, keeps every button and chip at least 44 px tall, and the app still installs on Android with its own name and icon.

## Decisions
-     One owner, one counterparty. The ledger is always between the signed-in owner and Abbaas; there is no screen to pick or add a counterparty.
-     Balance is in USD. A send adds its USD value; a repayment subtracts its USD value. A positive balance means Abbaas has the owner's money; a negative balance means the owner has Abbaas's money; zero is All square.
-     The balance amount is shown without a sign. Who holds the money is shown by position and colour: the owner's box is on the left and blue, Abbaas's box is on the right and teal; only the holder's box is filled, the other is a dashed outline showing $0. The same blue marks the owner's actions (Send button, send entries) and the same teal marks Abbaas's (Receive button, repayment entries).
-     Each entry stores its currency (USD or MVR), the amount in that currency, the rate when MVR (rufiyaa per 1 US dollar, typed by the owner), and its USD value. The USD value of an MVR entry is the amount divided by the rate, rounded half up to the cent, fixed when saved and never recomputed.
-     Money is stored as whole minor units (US cents, MVR laari); the rate is a decimal with up to four places. Totals and the balance are sums of stored USD cents.
-     Paid-to people are records owned by the account: a name, unique per account once normalised. The send form offers the saved people and creates a new one only when asked. An entry keeps the person's name as recorded, beside the identity of the record it was chosen from, so renaming a person later does not rewrite past entries.
-     Each entry has a calendar date (default today on the device, changeable) and a saved-at time. The list is grouped by date, newest date first, and newest saved first within a date.
-     The forms carry no note field, no after-saving preview, no quick amounts and no over-payment warning, as on the refined board. A repayment larger than what Abbaas holds still moves the balance to the owner's side once saved. Notes already stored stay in the database and are not shown.
-     There is no Saved message and no Undo after a save: the new entry's ringed card is the feedback. Removing a wrong entry is deferred to a later delete feature; the LedgerStore keeps removeEntry for it.
-     Backend: Supabase free tier, chosen over Cloudflare because it gives email sign-in and row-level security without writing a server. Postgres holds the data, Supabase Auth identifies the owner, row-level security limits every table to auth.uid(), and the browser calls Supabase directly with the JS client. A free project pauses after a week with no activity; that is acceptable for a ledger posted every few days, and it is restored from the dashboard.
-     Supabase schema lives in the repo as SQL migrations run by the Supabase CLI; local development and the acceptance suite use the CLI's local stack (Docker) so tests run against real Postgres, as in the meals project.
-     Sign-in happens once per device. The Supabase client keeps the session in the browser's storage and refreshes it automatically, so a returning visit, including one from the home-screen icon, opens Home without asking again. The sign-in screen appears only when there is no valid session.
-     Front end: a static, mobile-first TypeScript app built with Vite, no UI framework, served from GitHub Pages and built by a GitHub Actions workflow, laid out like the meals project (domain rules as pure functions, ports, a Supabase adapter, UI as functions from state to DOM).
-     There is no sign-up screen; the owner account is created in the Supabase dashboard. Only the anon key and project URL are given to the build; the service-role key is never in the repo.
-     Visual reference: the refined final canvas at https://claude.ai/artifact/XpzsEF5ovaU7cB3wYp5bEi as read on 2026-10-12 (artboards Main, Empty, YouHave, SendMvr, SendUsd, Saved, Receive, ReceiveOver, Expand, SaveSend, SaveReceive). The app matches it exactly. Fonts Manrope (titles, numbers) and Source Sans 3 (body); background #f4f7f6; owner blue #24508a with box tint #e7eef7; Abbaas teal #0f6f68 with box tint #e0f0ed; cards 14–22 px radius; touch targets at least 44 px.
-     Layout is fluid, not pinned to the 390 px artboard: from 360 px upward every screen fills the phone's width with no horizontal scrolling; above 560 px the app stays a centred column 560 px wide on the page background. Bottom buttons and the Saved message sit above env(safe-area-inset-bottom) so Android's gesture bar never covers them, and screen heights use dynamic viewport units so the address bar and keyboard do not hide content.
-     Motion uses one curve, cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1), with about 160 ms for number pops, 220–300 ms for colour, chips and the currency switch, 360–520 ms for entrances and the form reveal, and 700–900 ms for the balance count. With prefers-reduced-motion set, every screen shows its end state at once.
-     Android install: the site ships a web app manifest (name Abbaas Ledger, short name Ledger, display standalone, start URL the Pages path, background #f4f7f6, theme colour #f4f7f6, 192 px and 512 px icons including a maskable one) so Chrome offers Add to Home screen and the app opens full-screen. Ledger data still needs a connection; there is no offline entry.
-     Entry sides follow the money's owner on Home: a send is the owner's money going out, so it sits on the owner's (left) side of the centre line under You sent; a payment is Abbaas's money, so it sits on his (right) side under He paid back. This reverses version one.
-     The date on a form is a chip reading Today, <day> <month> (or the chosen date) with a calendar icon in the form's ink; tapping it opens the device's date picker, and the entry keeps that date.
